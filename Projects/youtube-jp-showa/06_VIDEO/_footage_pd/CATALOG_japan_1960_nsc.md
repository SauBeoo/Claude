## 1. Basics
- **Colour:** BLACK & WHITE throughout.
- **Narrator on camera:** YES. An American host stands on camera in a studio set (credited as John Secondari). The film is really the ABC News TV documentary **"JAPAN — ANCHOR IN THE EAST"** (title cards at 0 s, 1575 s and 3155 s; "ABC NEWS" credits 3160-3185 s). Most of the running time is the host in the studio plus long interviews, with a Western interviewer in shot.
- **Duration:** 3187 s (~53 min), 640x480, active area ~632:480:4:0.
- Only about 10-12 % of the film is usable clean B-roll: rush-hour trains, shipyard / car / toy / electronics factories, urban youth, family at home around an irori hearth plus a butsudan, tea ceremony, schoolchildren. The rallies are mostly the 1960 Anpo political protests, so use them with care.
- **NOT in the film:** offices / clerks, abacus, ledgers, banks, danchi / company housing, dormitories, baths, union notice boards, retirement ceremonies.
- No late-1940s occupation footage (no US soldiers, no ruins). Everything is ~1959-60.
- Cut times are ±1 s where checked at 1 s, ±2 s elsewhere. Trim 1 s at each end when cutting.
