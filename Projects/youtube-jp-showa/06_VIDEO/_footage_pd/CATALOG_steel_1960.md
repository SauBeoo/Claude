# CATALOG — steel_1960.mp4 (NARA ARC 642010, CC0)

Film on screen: **"CREATION OF NEW WEALTH"**. Photographed by Iwanami Productions for **Yawata Iron & Steel Co., Ltd.**, produced by Allegro Film Productions. Subject: building a new seaside steelworks (bay reclamation, pile/caisson foundations, blast-furnace erection, first ignition ceremony, first tapping, steelmaking and rolling).

## 1. Basics
- **Black & white**, sound. ~**1655 s** (last frame 1650 s = "THE END"). 640x480 with thin black side bars (crop 632:480:4:0).
- **No company housing, dormitories, danchi, homes, families, children, schools or offices with desks appear anywhere.** The film only shows the plant: construction, workers, machines, ceremony, furnace/mill. Housing and family shots will have to come from other films.
- Latin-letter text on screen besides the title cards: "DEMAG" on converters (820–869), "MESTA" on the mill (1040–44), "SACK" on a mill stand (990–94), "FUJI" on a gauge (1260), "B" on roll chocks (1493–96), "4M" on a depth scale (360), "D-25086" stamped on a tuyere (690). Other signs and boards are Japanese (kanji). Flag bunting including US and UK flags in the control room (1257–59).
- No Western people seen. No presenter.
- Ledger: `gov.archives.arc.642010` has one cut, **1093.5–1102.0** (video 11, st11_01_koujou_workers), marked **USED** below.

Timing: boundaries checked at 1-s steps are exact: 420–449, 656–687, 840–855, 1100–1119, 1195–1259, 1280–1295, 1325–1348, 1484–1499. All others come from the 5-s sheets and can be off by up to about 2 s.
