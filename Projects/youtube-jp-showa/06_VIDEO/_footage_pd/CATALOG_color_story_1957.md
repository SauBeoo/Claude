# CATALOG: "Color Story of Japan" (1957, US Navy, colour) - 2 reels

Source: NARA (archive.org item 428-npc-521 for R1). Federal work, public domain.
Ledger check (`_footage_test/FOOTAGE_USED.json`): this film is not in it, so nothing is marked USED.
Contact sheets: `scrub17/cs1`, `scrub17/cs2` (5 s), plus 1-2 s checks in `cs1f`, `cs1g`, `cs2a`-`cs2d`.

**What the film shows:** New Year shrine and temple visits (hatsumode), filmed in winter 1956/57, in Kamakura (Daibutsu, Tsurugaoka Hachimangu, Wakamiya-oji avenue, Kamakura station) and Tokyo (Asakusa Nakamise, Meiji Jingu, Imperial Palace plaza). It is almost all crowds, kimono, stalls, streets with 1950s cars and buses, and landscape.
**There are NO offices, desks, factories, machines, union meetings, company housing, danchi, laundry, baths, banks, or home interiors / family meals.** For video 17 it is useful only for city street, crowd, station/bus, family-with-children and faces B-roll.
Cut times are ±1-2 s where they fall between 5 s samples. Trim 1 s at each end when cutting.
