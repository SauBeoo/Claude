# SOURCES — PD/CC0 moving images of Japan 1945–1975 (archive.org sweep, 2026-09-23)

Method: ~40 archive.org `advancedsearch` queries (Japan/Japanese/Tokyo/Osaka/Kyoto/Tohoku/Hokkaido/Sendai/Aomori/rice/farm/factory/textile/railway/snow/Olympics/occupation, restricted to FedFlix · usgovfilms · wwIIarchive · universal_newsreels · prelinger · USIA `306-*` · USAF `342-USAF-*`), ~3,600 candidate items, then `archive.org/metadata/<id>` fetched for every row below.
License column = the item's `licenseurl` field copied verbatim. Duration = `length` of the listed mp4. **Nothing below was downloaded or scrubbed** — "what it shows" comes from the NARA shot-list / description text in the item, not from viewing.

Hard filters applied: PeriscopeFilm (BY-NC-ND or blank) excluded · items with **empty licenseurl AND empty rights** excluded (listed separately in §3) · `opensource_movies` self-uploads with self-applied PD marks excluded unless the file is a USIA/NARA record with its NARA ID quoted · Japanese commercial films excluded · WWII combat/war-crimes/atomic-medical excluded as off-theme.

## 4. Caveats (read before using)

- **A. Coverage gap — be honest about it.** No verifiably-PD item found for: 集団就職 trains from Ueno, cotton-spinning mills with young women, lathes/町工場, 1960s expressway construction, 出稼ぎ from snowy Tohoku, snowy rural stations, steam trains in snow, high-school entrance. The PD pool of **1955–1975 Japanese civilian life** on archive.org is essentially the CIA/NSC films (642010, 1692903, 1719752, 1991037) + Big Picture episodes + Color Story 1957 + a few Universal newsreels. For those themes you will likely need AI stills or other sources.
- **B. Compilation films may embed third-party footage.** "Japan" 1960 is an ABC News programme held by NSC/CIA; "Our Job in Japan" and some Big Picture episodes reuse captured/Japanese newsreel material. The item license covers the record, but third-party footage inside can carry separate rights — prefer shots clearly shot by US Army/USAF/Navy cameramen (the USAF-110xx and NPC rolls are raw government camera originals = safest).
- **C. 1964 Olympics newsreels:** Universal Newsreel is PD, but event footage of the Games may come from the Olympic organizing committee/IOC pool. Low risk for a few seconds of street/marathon, but don't build a segment on it.
- **D. PD mark on wwIIarchive/folkscanomy items** is applied by the uploader; the underlying films are US Air Force / Navy / Army Signal Corps records (NARA IDs quoted in descriptions), i.e. federal works — acceptable, but keep the NARA ID with each clip.
- **E. Found in this folder: `dokkoi_ikiteru_1951.webm`** = 今井正『どっこい生きてる』(1951, independent Japanese feature about ニコヨン). Under Japan's old Copyright Act a 1951 film by a named director runs until 38 years after the director's death if longer than the release term; 今井正 died 1991 → protection plausibly **until end of 2029**. It is a Japanese commercial feature and falls under your own exclusion — **do not use it** without a rights check.

## 5. Five best new items

1. **USAF11022 Japanese Industrial Life (1946)** — raw USAF camera original of Yawata steel & glass works: the cleanest PD factory/worker footage available, sister roll to 11068 you already trust.
2. **gov.archives.arc.1991037 Japan and Democracy (1949, CC0)** — the only PD film dated exactly 1949 about recovery and labour agitation; fits the 職安/ニコヨン opening era.
3. **NPC-14671 Ruins in Tokyo (1945)** — concrete working-class images (cart puller, family building a shack from wreckage) that set the "starting from zero, money-less" baseline.
4. **1946-06-20_Japan_Today (Universal Newsreel)** — 90 s that literally narrates black markets, inflation, poverty and meagre harvests; perfect money-theme cutaway.
5. **gov.archives.arc.2569464 Big Picture: Citizen, Soldier and Taxpayer, Too** — "Operation Rollup": Japanese workers in depot workshops rebuilding equipment, the Korean-war 特需 that first put wages back into working-class pockets (verify segment length when scrubbing).
